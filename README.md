# Karma Climb

Climb all the way to **Eclipse 16**, where every new level after 8 adds a level of Karma.

## Changes from Vanilla

* **Eclipse now goes up to 16**
  * Eclipse 9–16 keep **all eight Eclipse modifiers** and add a **Karma** level.<img width="1916" height="1074" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/35827b97-dbbb-4d3c-be92-e7dce1193908" />

  * **Karma is locked for the whole run.**<img width="850" height="879" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/be12cfbc-82bd-423c-ad99-5182fa0e461a" />

  * Unlocked just like vanilla Eclipse: beat Eclipse 8 with a survivor to unlock Eclipse 9 for them, beat 9 to unlock 10, and so on.
* **Karma reaches the whole run, not just Mithrix.** Each level adds to the ones before it:

  | Karma | Eclipse | Adds |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 9 | All enemies and bosses +10% damage. Kill gold +4% |
  | 2 | 10 | All enemies and bosses +15% attack speed. Kill gold +8% |
  | 3 | 11 | The teleporter starts with a Shrine of the Mountain stack (extra boss, extra item). Kill gold +12% |
  | 4 | 12 | Voidling, False Son and Solus Wing get a shell: after each 33% of health lost, 80% less damage for 5 seconds. Kill gold +16% |
  | 5 | 13 | Monster credits +20%. Kill gold +20%, cancelling Eclipse's −20% |
  | 6 | 14 | Voidtouched and Perfected elites can spawn on every stage |
  | 7 | 15 | Final bosses +30% health. Interactable credits +20% |
  | 8 | 16 | Final bosses spawn with a random elite aspect |

  * Mithrix keeps his vanilla Karma on top, except his damage and attack speed, which use the bonuses above instead.
* **Eclipse 1–8 no longer include Karma**
* **Eclipse menu**
  * A second row of medals for Eclipse 9–16: Karma blue once earned, and gold once every survivor has beaten that level, just like vanilla.<img width="884" height="203" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2bdbb671-cdf9-46d9-84f7-f4a3433a9b4a" />

  * New **Karma** box: hover a Karma badge to see exactly what that level adds, with Mithrix's changes listed separately.<img width="869" height="781" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4931150c-29e5-4090-a037-be2a0041bb45" />

  * The menu is a bit wider, and the difficulty description grows to fit the longer Eclipse 9–16 text.
  * Pressing **Escape** button takes you back to the main menu.
* Eclipse 9–16 show their own badge in character select and during the run.
* The new levels stay out of the normal lobby's difficulty picker, just like vanilla Eclipse.
